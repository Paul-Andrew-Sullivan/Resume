About Me

Hello! My name is Paul Andrew Sullivan, but I go by Andrew. I'm a senior Computer Science and Engineering student at the University of Iowa who likes building and tinkering.

I've always enjoyed learning how things work and improving them — from constructing with Legos as a kid to assembling my first custom PC in 8th grade. Over the years I've become the default IT consultant for family and friends, building custom systems and solving real-world tech problems.

Computers always came easier to me than other subjects, so CS&E was an easy pick. It lets me keep taking things apart to see how they work, both the hardware and the code.
